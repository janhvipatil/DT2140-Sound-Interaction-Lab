    • Shake up a gust

    Shake the phone to blow a gust of wind. Every shake starts a strong gust that then dies away over a couple of seconds, so a single shake is a short whoosh and continuous shaking keeps the storm going. The wind sound was written in Faust (faust-dsp/Nature/wind.dsp) and compiled to WebAssembly (wind.wasm) with the Faust toolchain.
