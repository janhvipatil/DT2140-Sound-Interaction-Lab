    • Shake to light the fire

    Shake the phone to light a fire, and keep shaking to stoke it. After the last shake the fire burns on for a few seconds and then slowly dies down, so you have to keep tending it. Turn the phone face down and the wood becomes wet: the fire spits with louder, sharper crackles. The fire is a Faust model (faust-dsp/Nature/fire.dsp) compiled to WebAssembly (fire.wasm).
