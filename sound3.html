    • Shake a horn call

    Shake the phone to blow a short brass note; keep shaking to hold the note, stop and it ends half a second later. Tilt the phone left or right while you play to change the lip tension of the virtual player, which bends the note, the same way tilting changes the bell's strike in Interaction 2.

    Developed by Maurizio Berta and Laura McHugh
    for DT2140 Multimodal Interfaces Sound Interaction Lab
    KTH Royal Institute of Technology, 2025
