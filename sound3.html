    • Tilt to blow

    The phone is a brass instrument. Lying flat it is silent; tilt the top edge up towards you to blow, and the further you tilt, the harder you blow and the louder and brassier the tone. Tilt left or right to bend the note, and shake to add a vibrato that fades away. On a computer, hold the mouse button: height controls the breath and left/right the note.
